# SCAR Model: Detailed Reference

## Table of Contents

## S - SAFE: Make the Decision Feel Low-Risk

**Purpose:** Reduce perceived risk so the executive feels comfortable approving.

### Elements to Incorporate

- **Familiar structures:** Reference known frameworks in the industry
- **Proven track records:** Cite case studies from comparable companies
- **Guardrails:** Include success metrics, go/no-go decision points, monitoring cadence

### Example

- **Before:** "We will implement a new sales approach"
- **After:** "We will adopt the Miller Heiman framework used by [comparable company], with stage reviews every 30 days to ensure we hit performance milestones"

### Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Referenced a known framework, methodology, or precedent?
- [ ] Included measurable checkpoints or decision gates?
- [ ] Mentioned a comparable success case?

## C - CREDIBLE: Show Business Understanding

**Purpose:** Demonstrate that you understand the business context, not just the technical solution.

### Elements to Incorporate

- **Acknowledge limitations:** Budget, capacity, dependencies
- **Articulate trade-offs:** What you prioritize and why
- **Connect to business objectives:** Link directly to strategic priorities

### Example

- **Before:** "This project will improve efficiency"
- **After:** "Given our goal to reduce operational costs by 20% this year, this reduces sales process expenses by $500K while preserving resources for Q3 product expansion"

### Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Acknowledged at least one real constraint?
- [ ] Stated a trade-off explicitly?
- [ ] Connected to a known strategic priority?

## A - ACTIONABLE: Communicate Intent and Outcomes

**Purpose:** Replace implementation details with clear outcomes and strategic intent.

### Elements to Incorporate

- **Defined outcomes** from Step 1 of the three-step system
- **Strategic intent:** Why this outcome matters now
- **Measurable success:** Exact metrics, timelines, accountable parties

### Example

- **Before:** "We will train the sales team on new objection handling techniques"
- **After:** "We will increase prospect-to-opportunity conversion rates from 12% to 18% by Q2, directly addressing our $2M growth gap"

### Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Stated the outcome, not the action?
- [ ] Explained why this matters now (strategic timing)?
- [ ] Included specific metrics and a timeline?

## R - REWIRE: Neutralize Objections

**Purpose:** Preemptively address objections identified in Step 2 before they are raised.

### Elements to Incorporate

- **Use objections from Step 2** of the three-step system
- **Address preventively:** Weave into the narrative before the audience raises them
- **Reframe the problem:** Shift the lens so the objection loses its power

### Example

- **Anticipated objection:** "We tried improving sales before and it didn't work"
- **Rewire response:** "Previous initiatives focused on skills training. Data shows our real problem is lead qualification, not sales technique. This approach targets the root cause that prior initiatives did not address"

### Rewire Techniques

| Technique | When to Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Root cause reframe | "We tried this before" objections | "Previous attempts addressed symptoms. This targets the root cause: X" |
| Data contradiction | False beliefs based on assumptions | "The assumption is X, but data from [source] shows Y" |
| Phased de-risking | "Too many resources" concerns | "Phase 1 requires only [minimal resource]. We expand only after proving [metric]" |
| Comparable proof | "This won't work here" skepticism | "[Similar company] faced the same constraint and achieved [result] in [timeframe]" |

### Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Identified the top 2-3 objections from Step 2?
- [ ] Addressed each one within the narrative (not as a separate objection-handling section)?
- [ ] Used reframing rather than direct contradiction?

## Applying SCAR Together

When rewriting a slide or section, apply all four elements. Not every element needs equal weight. Distribute emphasis based on context:

| Presentation Goal | Emphasize |
|---|---|
| Budget approval | Safe + Credible |
| Strategic alignment | Actionable + Credible |
| Progress update | Actionable + Safe |
| New initiative proposal | All four equally |
| Crisis response | Safe + Rewire |

After rewriting, verify each SCAR element is present by tagging them in the output.
